# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Keys to watch the next or previous character and to return to your own.
- Follows the watched character through doors.
- Mouse drags to raise, slide, zoom and turn the view.
- Free camera: fly anywhere, right click a character to watch them or a door
  to go through it.
- Your own character is put away while the camera is elsewhere, and can be
  brought to where the camera is.
- The minimap, sound, the game's character panel and Clothing State Menu follow
  the watched character.
